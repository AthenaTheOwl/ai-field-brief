# Editorial contract for the brief lenses

Read `VOICE.md` before drafting public copy. Read this contract before
running a lens. The source notes can be detailed; the digest still sounds
like a builder talking to another builder over coffee.

## Keep the evidence in order

- State what the source reports before drawing a conclusion. Distinguish
  a measured result, a vendor claim, a forecast, an illustration and the
  editor's inference. A release note records a fix; it does not count how
  often customers encountered the bug.
- Give a figure its unit, denominator, period and conditions. Keep price,
  revenue, demand, capacity and delivered output separate. A benchmark
  comparison needs its task set, harness and baseline. A fitted result
  needs a separate validation set before it becomes a validation result.
- Write the causal steps in order. Name the strongest competing account
  and the observation that would distinguish it. A correlation or a
  repeated anecdote can motivate the test without deciding its result.
- A transferable principle is a proposed transfer until checked in its
  new setting. Say where the analogy stops. Keep examples hypothetical
  unless the source or the author's supplied material records the event.
- A Try is a proposal, with a baseline, observable outcome and stop rule.
  Identify the cost and permission boundary. Passing a small fixture
  earns the next bounded step, not a claim about the whole deployment.

## Keep the author's choices

Preserve names, quotations, technical terms, dates, source links, dry
asides and the cadence that carries the point. Correct a factual error
with the smallest supported edit. Do not add a confession, interview,
experiment, measurement or story to complete a writing form.

Lead with the thing that happened. Put the inference after its evidence.
Keep a necessary limit beside the claim it limits. A short qualification
usually does the job; the reader did not order a second paragraph of
warnings. Vary sentence length when the thought needs it. End when the
last concrete move has landed.

## Check before handing off

Compare the revised claim with its source span and supporting cell.
Correct the cell as well when its meaning changed; record the new review
date without changing the original run's history. An unreachable source
is an access limit, not proof that its claim is false. Use a faithful
retained capture or remove unsupported precision and record the gap.

Run the evidence, field and voice gates, then read the prose in context.
A token match cannot certify faithfulness or humor. Keep an intentional
author choice when it earns its place; a narrow lint exception needs its
one-line reason. Do not weaken a gate or add a blanket exception.
