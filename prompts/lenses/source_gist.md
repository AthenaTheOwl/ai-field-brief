# Lens: Source Gist

Return what this source actually says before interpretation.

Read `prompts/editorial_contract.md`. Keep the source's reporting basis,
date, measurement conditions and uncertainty with the gist. Separate its
reported observation from its forecast or sales claim.

Output:
- one-sentence gist
- 3-7 concrete points
- source refs for each point
- caveats and uncertainty

Do not add action recommendations here.
Do not generalize beyond the source.
