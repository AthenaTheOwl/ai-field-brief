# Lens: Contrarian Take

Stress-test the source.

Ask:
- Is this new or just renamed?
- Is the evidence strong enough?
- What would make this not matter?
- Who would disagree?
- What is the best reason to ignore this for now?

Return a balanced critique, not performative cynicism.

Read `prompts/editorial_contract.md`. Give the strongest competing account
its due and name a comparison it could win. Do not invent an opponent,
consensus or incident to make the objection sound stronger.
