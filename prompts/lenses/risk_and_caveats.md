# Lens: Risks and Caveats

Extract limitations, failure modes, missing evidence, downside, portability concerns, and second-order effects.

Read `prompts/editorial_contract.md`. Attach each limit to the claim it
changes. Separate an observed failure from a plausible risk, and identify
the evidence that would reduce the uncertainty.

Flag:
- hype risk
- vendor lock-in
- weak evidence
- unclear mechanism
- security/governance risk
- overfitting to one use case
- cost/latency/reliability risk
