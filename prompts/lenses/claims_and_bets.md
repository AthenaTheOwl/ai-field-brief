# Lens: Claims and Bets

Extract explicit claims, forecasts, bets, disagreements, and open questions.

Distinguish:
- stated claim
- inferred claim
- prediction
- caveat
- contradiction

Preserve uncertainty. Never convert a narrow claim into broad consensus.

Read `prompts/editorial_contract.md`. Give each quantitative claim its
unit, denominator, period and comparison. Keep a quoted forecast distinct
from the editor's expectation and from an observed result.
