# Lens: Watchlist Trigger

Define whether and when to revisit this item.

Read `prompts/editorial_contract.md`. Name an observable upgrade trigger
and a demotion condition. Date-sensitive forecasts keep their original
time window; do not quietly roll the deadline forward after a miss.

Output:
- monitor reason
- revisit trigger
- likely timeframe
- what evidence would upgrade it to prototype/adopt
- what evidence would downgrade it to archive/reject
